**比赛场地&规则**

  

![](images/img-4.png)

经过周密的设计与搭建，机械赛的场地最终完成。

比赛核心为四重关卡，依次为移动挑战、蓝虎物流、建筑大师和物块堆放。

![](images/img-2.png)

After careful design and construction, the venue of the mechanical competition was finally completed.

At the heart of the competition are four levels, which in turn are the Moving Challenge, the Transport Challenge, the Master Builder and the Block Stacking

![](images/img-5.png)

![](images/img-6.jpg)

初赛赛制为积分制，每个关卡每个任务都有对应的分值，将分值累加即为单场得分。每个组赛三场，三场结束后比较三场累加得分，按得分高低排名。

![](images/img-2.png)

The competition system  is points system. Each level and each task has a corresponding point value, which is added up to a single game score. Each group has three games, and after the end of the three games, the cumulative score of the three games is compared, and the ranking is according to the high score.

![](images/img-7.png)

  

**比赛过程**

  

![](images/img-4.png)

4月13日8:00，在龙宾楼唐君远学生创新创业中心，机械赛初赛正式开始。共有36支队伍参加比赛。其中29支队伍实现了基本功能，成功完赛。

![](images/img-2.png)

At 8:00 on April 13, Tang Junyuan Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center, the preliminary mechanical competition began. A total of thirty-six teams participated in the competition. twenty-nine teams Among them achieved basic functions and successfully completed the competition.

![](images/img-8.jpg)

各支队伍发挥自己天马行空的想象力，搭建了独具一格的机械结构，展示了各不相同的致胜策略。

![](images/img-2.png)

Every team used their imaginations to build unique mechanical structures and display different winning strategies.

![](images/img-7.png)

  

**初赛结果**

  

![](images/img-4.png)

经过激烈的比拼，一共有16支队伍进入复赛。其中Five4，Cyberkobe和横空出逝队暂居积分榜前三。

![](images/img-2.png)

After a fierce competition, a total of 16 teams entered the semifinals. Five4, Cyberkobe and Hengkongchushi occupy the top three positions in the standings.
